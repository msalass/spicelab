Applied Geochemistry

South Pacific Isotope Centre (SPICe Lab)

Dr. Marcos Salas-Saavedra · PhD, University of Queensland · Postdoc, Princeton University

We use isotopes and elements as tracers to solve environmental, agricultural, and geological resource challenges.

SPICe Lab is a developing geochemistry center in southern Chile, focused on using isotopes, trace elements, and microscale chemistry to support decision-making in environmental, agricultural, and natural resource matters.

We help researchers, companies, and institutions interpret geochemical signals, design sampling strategies, and connect measurements with real processes in soils, waters, carbonates, and mineral systems.

How can we support you today?

While SPICe Lab's own analytical facilities are under development, we already offer consulting services, data interpretation, and project design, working with an international network of laboratories when high-precision measurements are required.

Geochemical Consulting

For research groups, companies, and public institutions.

Data Interpretation

Isotopic and elemental data from waters, soils, carbonates, and mineral systems.

Project Design

Sampling strategies for environmental, agricultural, and resource studies.

Report Review

Isotopic ratios, trace elements, rare earths, and analytical quality assessment.

Technical Advisory

Analytical technique selection and international sample coordination.

Analysis & Visualization

Turning measurements into actionable information for decision-making.

Laser Microanalysis

Consulting on laser ablation methods, QA/QC strategies, and results interpretation.

Who we work with

We add value across sectors by connecting advanced science with real industry and academic needs.

Academia

Universities and research groups requiring high-precision data for publications and studies.

Mining & Exploration

Lithium and strategic mineral companies needing geochemical fingerprints for exploration or production.

Agriculture

Producers, soil labs, and advisors seeking to understand nutrient cycles and soil-plant interactions.

Environment

Consultants, NGOs, and public agencies focused on water resources, pollution, and land use.

Analytical workflow at this stage

We operate under a hybrid model: we provide the scientific intelligence and guarantee analytical quality through our global network.

01

Design & Strategy

We define with you which isotopes or elements best answer your question and design the sampling (SPICe Lab).

02

Sample Management

We coordinate physical preparation and secure shipping to certified international laboratories.

03

International Analysis

Measurement at world-class collaborating facilities (while we build our own lab).

04

Interpretation

We transform raw data into digestible geochemical reports useful for decision-making (SPICe Lab).
