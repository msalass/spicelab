About SPICe Lab

A developing centre for isotopes and trace elements in southern Chile, designed to connect advanced geochemistry with real-world problems.

The South Pacific Isotope Centre (SPICe Lab) is a developing initiative to bring expertise in isotopes and trace elements to the environmental, agricultural, and natural resource challenges of southern Chile and the South Pacific region.

Mission

Our mission is to help researchers, companies, and institutions use isotopes and elemental signatures as tracers to understand how natural and industrial systems behave, interact, and change: from water and soils to carbonates, mineral resources, and critical materials.

Vision

SPICe Lab is developing as a bridge between advanced isotopic geochemistry and practical decision-making in agriculture, environmental management, and resource development. In the long term, we aim to establish a regional centre in southern Chile that:

  • Supports high-quality geochemical work in the South Pacific region.
  • Connects academia, industry, and public institutions.
  • Trains new professionals in isotope-based approaches.

Expertise & Foundations

This expertise comes from work in international research environments and collaborations with universities and laboratories in various countries. SPICe Lab seeks to translate this background into applied support for projects in Chile and the region.

Isotopic Geochemistry

Specialization in carbonate and mineral systems.

Trace Elements

Analysis of trace elements and rare earths for traceability.

Microanalysis

Microanalytical techniques and high-resolution laser ablation.

Reconstruction

Environmental and paleoenvironmental reconstruction through geochemical archives.

Why Southern Chile?

Southern Chile is characterized by:

  • Accelerated environmental and climate changes.
  • Intensive agricultural and forestry activity.
  • Growing interest in critical materials and mineral resources.

Developing SPICe Lab in this region brings isotope and trace element tools closer to local questions, while maintaining connections with international analytical infrastructure.
