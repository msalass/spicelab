About SPICe Lab

Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality isotope and trace-element analyses that help researchers, industry and public institutions understand environmental change, water and soil processes, and the behaviour of critical raw materials.

Vision

We aim to become a reference isotope centre in the South Pacific region, connecting world-class analytical methods with local and regional challenges in climate, environment, agriculture and resources.

Our story

SPICe Lab was founded by researchers with experience in carbonate systems, sea-level and climate reconstructions, and isotope geochemistry. After working abroad in international laboratories, we chose southern Chile as a base to develop a laboratory that combines advanced analytical capabilities with strong regional focus.

The South Pacific is a key region for understanding climate variability, ocean–continent interactions and the impact of human activities. SPICe Lab exists to support this work with robust, interpretable geochemical data.

Team

Our core team brings together expertise in isotope geochemistry, environmental sciences and analytical instrumentation. This section will grow as new collaborators, students and staff join SPICe Lab.

  • Scientific direction: Isotope geochemistry, carbonate systems, U–Th dating.
  • Technical support: Sample preparation, clean lab procedures, laser ablation and mass spectrometry.
  • Collaborators: Partner researchers in universities and research centres.

Collaborations and partners

SPICe Lab is open to collaboration with universities, research institutes, companies and public agencies. We are interested in projects related to:

  • Paleoclimate and sea-level change
  • Water resources and catchment processes
  • Soils, agriculture and biogeochemical cycles
  • Lithium, critical metals and mineral resources

If you would like to explore a collaboration or develop a joint project, please get in touch.
