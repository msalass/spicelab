Services

SPICe Lab offers isotope and trace-element analysis, method development and scientific consulting. We work with researchers, industry and public institutions to design and execute analytical strategies that answer specific questions.

Isotope geochronology

We provide U–Th based geochronology for carbonate materials and related archives.

  • Corals and reef frameworks
  • Speleothems (cave carbonates)
  • Travertines and tufas
  • Meteoric calcite and diagenetic carbonates

Deliverables: age estimates with uncertainties, quality control metrics and basic age–depth models where appropriate.

Environmental and agricultural isotopes

Isotopic tools to understand water, soil and biogeochemical processes.

  • Tracing sources and pathways of water and solutes
  • Characterising soil and sediment processes
  • Evaluating land-use and management impacts
  • Supporting monitoring programs and experiments

Lithium, REEs and critical raw materials

Geochemical and, where available, isotopic characterisation for exploration and process monitoring.

  • Brines, waters and process fluids
  • Rocks, clays and mineral concentrates
  • Support for baseline studies and QA/QC programs

Laser ablation and micro-analysis

High-resolution elemental profiles and maps in solid materials.

  • Coral and shell transects
  • Mineral zoning and textures
  • Teeth, bones and other biogenic archives

Deliverables: processed datasets, basic visualisations and a summary of analytical conditions.

Consulting and training

We also support projects through:

  • Study design and sampling strategies
  • Selection of appropriate analytical approaches
  • Interpretation of isotope and trace-element data
  • Short courses and workshops for research groups or industry teams
