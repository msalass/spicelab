Services & Capabilities

Consulting, interpretation and analytical strategy in isotope and trace-element geochemistry.

SPICe Lab supports researchers, companies and public institutions that need to turn geochemical data into decisions. At this stage, our work is centred on consulting, data interpretation and project design, with laboratory measurements carried out through partner institutions in Chile and abroad.

What we can offer right now

The services below are available immediately and can be adapted to research, exploration or monitoring contexts in environment, agriculture and mineral resources:

  • Geochemical consulting to explore whether isotopes and trace elements can help answer your questions.
  • Interpretation of isotopic and elemental data from waters, soils, carbonates and mineral systems.
  • Project design and sampling strategy for field campaigns, monitoring networks and experimental studies.
  • Review of laboratory reports and datasets, including cross-checks, uncertainty awareness and quality flags.
  • Selection of analytical techniques (e.g. ICP-MS, MC-ICP-MS, LA-ICP-MS, stable isotopes) and where to run them.
  • Data analysis, visualisation and reporting for articles, theses, internal reports or decision-making documents.
  • Laser ablation and micro-analysis consulting on method development, mapping strategies and QA/QC approaches.

Analytical measurements and partner laboratories

Most geochemical analyses associated with SPICe Lab projects are currently performed in established international laboratories and collaborating facilities. Rather than duplicating existing infrastructure, we focus on:

  • identifying the most suitable laboratory and analytical approach for your samples,
  • helping prepare and document samples correctly,
  • coordinating analytical runs where appropriate, and
  • interpreting the results in the context of your project.

As SPICe Lab’s own facilities are installed in southern Chile, in-house analytical capabilities will be added to complement this network and better serve regional needs.

Key focus areas

SPICe Lab is being developed around several application areas where isotopes and elements are particularly powerful tools:

  • Critical materials (Li, REEs, strategic minerals) – geochemical fingerprinting, exploration support and process monitoring in brines, ores and processing streams.
  • Agriculture and soils – nutrient cycles, soil–plant interactions, fertiliser use and land-use impacts viewed through elemental and isotopic signatures.
  • Environmental isotopes – tracing water pathways, sources of contamination and the behaviour of pollutants in catchments and aquifers.
  • Carbonates and mineral systems – using carbonate cements, speleothems and other minerals as archives of environmental and diagenetic processes.
  • Laser ablation and micro-analysis – spatially resolved elemental data at the grain or crystal scale to reveal growth histories and fluid–rock interaction.
  • U–Th geochronology (planned capability) – at present carried out in international laboratories, with SPICe Lab providing support for sample selection, data evaluation and age interpretation.

How a typical engagement works

Every project is different, but most collaborations follow a structure similar to this:

  • 1. Initial conversation – we clarify your questions, data available and constraints.
  • 2. Review of background and datasets – existing information is compiled and assessed.
  • 3. Proposal and scope – you receive a clear outline of tasks, timelines and expected outcomes.
  • 4. Implementation – consulting, data analysis and coordination with analytical labs as agreed.
  • 5. Reporting and follow-up – results are presented in a format that you can use and revisit.
