Sample submission

This page summarises how to prepare and send samples to SPICe Lab. As procedures become final, you can add downloadable forms and detailed protocols.

1. Initial contact

Before sending any samples, please contact us to discuss your project, analytical needs and timelines. This helps ensure that sample types and quantities are appropriate for the methods to be used.

2. Sample information

For each sample, we recommend providing at least:

  • Sample ID and short description
  • Location and basic context (e.g. depth, formation, site)
  • Requested analyses (e.g. U–Th ages, trace elements, etc.)
  • Any relevant safety or handling notes

3. Packaging and shipping

Samples should be packaged to avoid breakage and contamination. In general:

  • Use sturdy containers and avoid loose material inside boxes.
  • Label containers and bags clearly with sample IDs.
  • Include a printed list of all samples in the shipment.

Shipping details (addresses and carrier recommendations) will be provided once a project has been agreed.

4. Forms and templates

In the future, this section can include a downloadable PDF or spreadsheet for sample submission. For now, please summarise sample information in a spreadsheet and share it with us by email.

If you have specific questions about sample preparation or shipping, please contact us.
