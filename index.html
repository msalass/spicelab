Applied Geochemistry

South Pacific Isotope Centre (SPICe Lab)

We use isotopes and elements as tracers to solve environmental, agricultural, and geological resource challenges.

SPICe Lab is a developing geochemistry center in southern Chile, focused on using isotopes, trace elements, and microscale chemistry to support decision-making in environmental, agricultural, and natural resource matters.

We help researchers, companies, and institutions interpret geochemical signals, design sampling strategies, and connect measurements with real processes in soils, waters, carbonates, and mineral systems.

Key focus areas

SPICe Lab is being designed around the following areas of isotopic and trace element geochemistry:

Critical Materials

Geochemical fingerprints of Li, rare earths, and strategic minerals in brines, ores, and related materials.

Agriculture & Soils

Tracing nutrient cycles and soil-plant interactions with elemental and isotopic tools.

Environmental Isotopes

Understanding water pathways, pollution sources, and sedimentary or carbonate archives.

Laser Ablation

High-resolution elemental mapping and in-situ chemistry in carbonates, mineral grains, and industrial materials.

Isotopic Tracers

Linking geochemical signals with sources, processes, and environmental change.

U-Th Geochronology

Available through international laboratories, with our support in sample selection and interpretation.

Analytical workflow at this stage

We operate under a hybrid model: we provide the scientific intelligence and guarantee analytical quality through our global network.

01

Design & Strategy

We define with you which isotopes or elements best answer your question and design the sampling (SPICe Lab).

02

Sample Management

We coordinate physical preparation and secure shipping to certified international laboratories.

03

International Analysis

Measurement at world-class collaborating facilities (while we build our own lab).

04

Interpretation

We transform raw data into digestible geochemical reports useful for decision-making (SPICe Lab).
