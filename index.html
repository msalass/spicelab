South Pacific Isotope Centre (SPICe Lab)

High-precision isotope geochemistry for environment, agriculture and critical resources.

SPICe Lab is an emerging geochemistry centre currently being developed in southern Chile, with the goal of providing high-quality isotope and trace-element analyses for researchers, companies and public institutions in the South Pacific region and beyond.

As the laboratory and facilities come online, SPICe Lab will support projects that connect advanced isotope tools with real environmental, agricultural and resource challenges.

Planned services

What we focus on

As SPICe Lab is established, it is being designed to focus on the following core areas of isotope and trace-element geochemistry:

  • Isotope geochronology – U–Th dating and related techniques for corals, speleothems, travertines and other carbonate archives.
  • Environmental and agricultural isotopes – tools to understand water, soils, nutrient cycles and land-use impacts.
  • Lithium, REEs and critical raw materials – geochemical and isotopic characterization to support exploration and process monitoring.
  • Laser ablation and micro-analysis – high-resolution elemental maps and profiles in minerals, corals and other materials.

Who we aim to work with

SPICe Lab is being built to collaborate with institutions and organisations that need robust, interpretable geochemical data:

  • Universities and research centres
  • Mining and lithium companies
  • Agricultural and environmental organisations
  • Government agencies and NGOs

How SPICe Lab is being designed

SPICe Lab builds on existing experience in isotope geochemistry and carbonate systems, and is being designed with the following principles in mind:

  • High analytical quality – methods based on international best practices and reference materials.
  • Expert interpretation – experience in carbonate systems, climate, water and critical metals.
  • Regional focus, global standards – a lab located in southern Chile, connected to international networks.
  • Transparent data – clear reports, uncertainty estimates and open communication.

Current status

SPICe Lab is currently in the development and installation phase. Some of the services and capabilities described on this site represent planned activities that will come online as the laboratory becomes fully operational.

Interested in future collaboration?

If you are planning a research project, an exploration campaign or a monitoring program and would like to explore how isotope analyses could support your work, we would be glad to hear from you as SPICe Lab develops.
